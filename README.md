AcuShield Mobile

AI-Powered Women's Safety Alert and Digital Evidence Protection System

Sustainable Development Goal (SDG 5: Gender Equality)

AcuShield Mobile is an AI-powered women's safety application designed to provide immediate assistance during emergency situations. The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, GPS tracking, emergency communication, voice recognition, and secure evidence protection to enhance women's safety and security.

---

Project Objective

The primary objective of AcuShield Mobile is to improve women's personal safety by providing intelligent emergency response mechanisms, real-time location sharing, automatic threat detection, and secure evidence preservation.

---

Problem Statement

Women often face unsafe situations in public transportation, educational institutions, workplaces, and isolated locations. Traditional safety solutions rely heavily on manual actions, which may not be possible during emergencies.

AcuShield Mobile addresses this challenge by providing AI-powered emergency detection, rapid communication, GPS tracking, and digital evidence protection.
